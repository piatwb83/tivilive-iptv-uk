## Is IPTV UK legal?

This is the part worth keeping clear.

**IPTV is a way of delivering television over the internet.** The legal issue is whether the content being streamed is properly authorised. UK government guidance describes illicit streaming as accessing copyrighted content without the rights holder's permission, including through TVs, computers, phones and streaming apps.

TiviLive's terms describe the company as a reseller and say that content is supplied through the service, but the public pages reviewed here do not provide a channel-by-channel licensing breakdown for the premium UK services listed in its directory.

That means UK customers should verify that the particular content they intend to watch is authorised for distribution before paying for a subscription. This is especially relevant when a service advertises access to premium sports and subscription television categories.
